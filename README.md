# **Association of maternal and fetal compartment-specific miRNA and T-cell co-signalling profiles in gestational diabetes mellitus**

## Overview

This repository contains R scripts associated with the manuscript **“Association of maternal and fetal compartment-specific miRNA and T-cell co-signalling profiles in gestational diabetes mellitus.”**

The study examines T-cell co-signalling phenotypes and microRNA (miRNA) expression in maternal peripheral blood, umbilical cord blood, and decidual tissue from pregnancies with gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) and controls. It also explores associations across compartments and reanalyzes a public placental single-cell RNA-sequencing dataset to investigate differentially expressed genes that are experimentally validated miRNA targets.

## Analyses

The scripts cover:

- Clinical and pregnancy-related variables.
- Flow-cytometry phenotypes and miRNA expression.
- Correlations within and between biological compartments.
- Sensitivity analyses of statistical models.
- Reanalysis of publicly available placental single-cell RNA-seq data.
